 ### 1. QR Code Scanner

- Open camera and scan a QR code containing a table ID (format: `ipot://table/{tableId}`)
- Handle invalid QR codes gracefully
- After scanning, navigate to the menu for that table's restaurant

### 2. Menu Browser

- Fetch menu from API: `GET /api/v1/menu?table_id={tableId}`
- Display menu categories as tabs/sections
- Show menu items with: name, description, price, image (if available)
- Support searching/filtering menu items

### 3. Cart & Customizations

- Add items to cart with quantity controls (+/-)
- Support item customizations (e.g., "No onion", "Extra spicy", "Medium rare")
    - Each customization may have a price modifier
- Show cart summary with item count, subtotal, and customization details
- Allow removing items or adjusting quantities
